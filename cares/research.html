Computational Design of Novel Materials

We use quantum mechanical and atomistic simulations to understand how catalysts work, and use that understanding to screen and design materials that turn CO2, methane and biomass into cleaner fuels and chemicals.

  • DFT
  • Ab initio & Car-Parrinello MD
  • MLIP
  • Microkinetic modeling
  • Multiscale modeling
