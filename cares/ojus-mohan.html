Dr. Ojus Mohan

Assistant ProfessorIndian Institute of Technology Bombay

Dr. Ojus Mohan is an Assistant Professor of Chemical Engineering at IIT Bombay, where he leads the CARES Lab. The group designs sustainable, efficient and stable catalysts using quantum mechanical simulations (DFT), machine-learned interatomic potentials, microkinetic and multiscale modeling, with a focus on CO2 conversion and heterogeneous catalysis. Before joining IIT Bombay, he was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Alberta, and he received his Ph.D. from NTU Singapore.
